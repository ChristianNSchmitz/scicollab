BEFORE YOU MAKE THIS PUBLIC DS-1174 · first public posting from this project
Publishing this destroys your ability to patent it in Europe.
Under the European Patent Convention, any public disclosure before a patent application is filed becomes prior art against your own invention. It destroys novelty. There is no general grace period in Europe — unlike the United States, where a twelve-month grace period exists, in Europe the loss is immediate and cannot be undone by filing later.
WHAT COUNTS AS DISCLOSURE
Making this dataset, method card or figure public here
A preprint, a conference poster, a talk with slides, a thesis in a library
A tweet describing the result in enough detail to enable it
Sharing with a named collaborator under a confidentiality agreement does not count. Lab and Institution visibility on this platform do not count.

WHY THIS MODAL EXISTS
Loss of patent rights through premature disclosure is one of the most common and most expensive mistakes in academic research, and it is always made by someone who did not know the rule rather than someone who weighed it. A researcher publishing a dataset at 23:00 is not thinking about the EPC. This is the last moment anyone can tell them.
THE ASYMMETRY IS DELIBERATE
Keep private is the primary: full width, dark, one click. The reversible choice should be the easy one.
Publish anyway requires typing a word. Not to obstruct — to convert an automatic click into a decision the person will remember making.
Both outcomes are written to the audit log with the text shown, so the institution can later demonstrate the researcher was warned.
SHOWN ONCE PER PROJECT, NOT PER FILE
On the first public posting from a project only. A gate that appears on every upload is a gate people learn to dismiss without reading, which is worse than no gate at all.
