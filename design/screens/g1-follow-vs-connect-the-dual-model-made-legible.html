CONNECT symmetric · they must accept · unlocks sharing
A request they accept or ignore. Accepting makes you mutually visible and opens the private channel — this is the edge that actually carries work between labs.
Grants both of you
direct messages without the reputation gate · the ability to share a Lab-visibility artifact one-to-one · warm-introduction paths through each other
Does not grant
access to your projects · your lab's data · anything your institution governs. A connection is a person-to-person edge, never an access-control decision.
Visible to
the two of you · connection lists are private by default, because they map collaborations that are not yet public
Either side can disconnect. The other is not notified, and any artifact already shared stays shared — revoking access is a separate, deliberate act on the artifact itself.
WHY TWO EDGES, NOT ONE
A single "connect" button forces every relationship into a negotiation. Most scientific attention is one-directional — you read someone's work for years without meeting them — and pretending otherwise produces the inbox of requests that makes existing networks unusable.
