THE PRIVACY DECISION, ON THE SCREEN
We do not record who reads your work. Reader identity is discarded at the edge and never written to storage — what is kept is an institution and a country, and only where at least three distinct readers from that institution have accessed the item in the period.
Why: a researcher who knows their browsing is logged reads less, and reads more defensively. The chilling effect costs more than the analytics are worth.
Consequence: you can never learn that a specific rival read your dataset. That is the trade, stated plainly, and it is not configurable.
