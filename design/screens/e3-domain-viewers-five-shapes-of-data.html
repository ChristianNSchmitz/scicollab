WHAT ALL FIVE VIEWERS SHARE
01The same chrome: visibility chip, format and provenance line, and the same control row position. Learning one teaches you all five.
02Every viewer accepts an anchored annotation — an x/y/z coordinate, a ppm value, a base range, a map polygon, a time window — so the adjacent discussion can point at something precise.
03Each states a will not. A viewer that quietly segments, aligns, interpolates or kriges produces numbers with no recorded method, which is the opposite of what this platform is for.
04Nothing a viewer computes is written back to the dataset. Your integrals and selections are yours; the deposited bytes do not move.
WHY NOT ONE UNIVERSAL VIEWER
Because the five shapes disagree about what an axis is. A generic table-and-chart viewer would render a z-stack as 18 rows and an NMR spectrum as 32,768 numbers — technically correct and useless at the bench. Five viewers is more surface to maintain and the right trade.
