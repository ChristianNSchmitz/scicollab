TAG WIKIv7 · 2026-07-14 · history
Targeted insertion of a defined sequence at a specified genomic locus using a CRISPR nuclease and a homology-directed repair template. Use this tag for insertion; use crispr-knockout for disruption, and base-editing where no double-strand break is made.
WHEN NOT TO USE THIS TAG
Do not use for transient expression experiments, or for prime editing. Both were mis-tagged here for two years and produced a search corpus that mixed three different techniques.
