KNOWN FAILURE MODES · 3each one is a real run someone recorded
SAMPLE
Passage above 18 — efficiency falls to 4 ± 1%. Cooperative uptake collapses with receptor loss; not recoverable by reagent change.
confirmed by 2 independent reproductions · Q-2291
PROTOCOL
Serum-free medium through incubation — below 5%. Serum-free belongs to step 3 only.
EQUIPMENT
CO₂ drift below 4.5% mimics the passage effect. Log it before blaming the cells.
