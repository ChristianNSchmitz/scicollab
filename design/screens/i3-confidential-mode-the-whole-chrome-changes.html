WHY THE ENTIRE CHROME CHANGES
A researcher working across a public tenant and a confidential one in the same afternoon will paste the wrong thing into the wrong window if the two look alike. The dark blue ground, the permanent header bar and the changed accent are all one signal: you are somewhere else, and the rules are different.
WHO USES THIS
Industry-sponsored work under a contract that forbids disclosure, pre-patent research, clinical data under a restrictive DPA, and defence-adjacent work subject to export control. Roughly one lab in eight at a research university.
SWITCHING A TENANT
Standard → confidential is available and takes effect immediately. Confidential → standard is not self-service — it needs a signed instruction from the institution, because the reverse direction can expose work that was created on a promise it never would be.
