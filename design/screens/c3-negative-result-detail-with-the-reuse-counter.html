Neither reagent lot nor plasmid quality rescues knock-in efficiency above passage 18

A. Rivera · published 2026-09-02 · HEK293T · n = 9 wells · confidence: solid
HYPOTHESIS
Efficiency is limited by lipid-complex quality, so a fresh lot and fresh prep should restore it at any passage.
WHAT HAPPENED
4 ± 1% across nine wells, two lots, three thaws. Viability above 92%. Nothing changed.
