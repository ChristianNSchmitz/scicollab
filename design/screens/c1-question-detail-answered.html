It is the cells, not the medium or the plasmid. HEK293T progressively lose the surface receptor that lipid complexes depend on; the drop is steep rather than gradual because uptake is cooperative. I saw exactly this at p17 in 2024 — MC-0288 v6 records it, and its fails under section names passage > 18.
