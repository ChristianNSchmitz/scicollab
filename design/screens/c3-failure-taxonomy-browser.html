WHY A TAXONOMY AND NOT TAGS
Tags describe what a result is about. The taxonomy describes what broke. Six causes, fixed, so a failure filed in one lab is findable by a lab that would never have used the same words for it.
THE BRANCH WE WATCH
Sample is the largest branch and the one where duplicated work is most expensive — a mis-identified line invalidates everything downstream. It is also where a single well-powered null result saves the most weeks.
