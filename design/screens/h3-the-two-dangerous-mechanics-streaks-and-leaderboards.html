13CONTRIBUTION STREAK · K01OFF
Show me a contribution streak
A consecutive-day counter on your dashboard, visible only to you.
READ THIS BEFORE TURNING IT ON
Loss aversion makes streaks the strongest retention mechanic known. In research it is actively harmful: it rewards daily output, and good science is intermittent. A week of reading, a month of a failing experiment, and a fortnight of writing are all zero-activity periods that produce the best work.
IF YOU DO TURN IT ON
Private. It never appears on your public profile.
Never linked to publication output — no streak counts papers.
A broken streak is shown as a gap, not a failure. No "you lost your streak", no flame going out.
No streak leaderboards exist, at any scope.
Squares are weighted by contribution type, so a trivial edit does not look like a replication.
19LEADERBOARD · K05OFF · OPT-IN PER COMMUNITY
WHY THIS IS NOT GLOBAL AND NOT PUBLICATION-BASED
A global leaderboard ranked on output is the most dangerous mechanic available to a research platform: it rewards measurable output over correct output. The person who publishes six thin papers outranks the person who spent the year replicating someone else's result and finding it did not hold. So: opt-in only, scoped to a single community, and never based on publication counts.
