COMMERCIAL FEATURE · RECRUITER SEAT
RECRUITERS CANNOT SEARCH THE MEMBERSHIP
They can only search the 412 people who explicitly opted in to being found for opportunities. The other 22,000 accounts are not merely hidden from the results — they are not in the searchable set at all, and no filter combination reveals a count of them.

EVERY CONTACT IS LOGGED AND VISIBLE TO THE CANDIDATE
"Novartis viewed your profile via recruiter search on 14 September" appears in the candidate's own activity, with a one-click block. The asymmetry of recruiter tooling is the thing that makes people leave professional networks.
Rate limits apply. Twenty approaches per recruiter per month, and a recruiter whose messages are reported twice loses the seat. The quarantine applies to them exactly as it does to anyone else.
