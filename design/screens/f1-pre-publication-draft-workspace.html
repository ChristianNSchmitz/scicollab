RESULTS · PARAGRAPH 2
Knock-in efficiency was stable at 41 ± 3% through passage 16 and collapsed to 4 ± 1% at passage 19 (Figure 2). Viability was unchanged across the series, so the effect is not attributable to cell death. Two reagent lots and a fresh plasmid preparation were tested and neither restored efficiency, which rules out reagent quality as the cause (Rivera, 2026). All gating was performed with gate-and-quantify v1.4.0 following the protocol recorded as MC-0288 v6, unmodified.
