WHAT OTHERS MAY DO WITH THIS DATA
Use it for anything, as long as they credit youCC-BY-4.0
Including commercially, including in a paper that disagrees with yours. They must cite the DOI. This is what most funders now require.
Use it for anything, no conditions at allCC0-1.0
You waive attribution. Citation still happens in practice, and this maximises reuse — but you cannot require credit.
Credit you, and not sell itCC-BY-NC-4.0
Blocks commercial reuse — which in practice also blocks some university spin-outs and many meta-analyses. Does not satisfy your FWO award.
Nothing without asking me firstcontrolled access
Metadata stays public and citable; files require a request and a data use agreement. For anything with a re-identification risk.
