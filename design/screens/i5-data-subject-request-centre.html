THE CLOCK IS THE DESIGN
One month from receipt, Art. 12(3). It is on every row, in the list and in the detail, counting in days rather than as a date — because "day 19 of 30" prompts action and "due 21 September" does not.
WHO IS THE CONTROLLER
For institutional accounts the university is the controller and SciCollab is the processor — so requests land here, in your console, not in our support queue. For personal accounts we are the controller and they land with us. The screen states which regime applies to each request.
