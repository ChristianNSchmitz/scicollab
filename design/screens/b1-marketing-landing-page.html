FIRST-CLASS OBJECT
Negative and null results

Not a footnote. Not a graveyard section. A citable, reusable object with the same weight as a positive result — and its own scored axis on your profile. Orange, deliberately not red: a failure that reproduces is a contribution.

FIRST-CLASS OBJECT
Method Cards

Structured, versioned, forkable records of how something was actually done — including the conditions under which it failed. Fork one, run it, and your result attaches to the card whether it worked or not.
