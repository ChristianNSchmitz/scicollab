Export for a funder
These are institution-level indicators. They are not designed for evaluating individual staff, and the data to do so is not produced.
There is no per-person breakdown behind a permission or an export — the aggregation happens before storage, and any group smaller than five people reports as a range rather than a figure. If someone asks you for "the numbers for Dr X", the honest answer is that this system cannot produce them, and that is deliberate.

WHAT IS NOT ON THIS SCREEN

WHY WE HOLD THIS LINE
A researcher who believes the platform feeds their appraisal stops recording honest null results within a month — and null results are the differentiator. Refusing individual metrics is not only an ethics position; it is what keeps the data worth having.
