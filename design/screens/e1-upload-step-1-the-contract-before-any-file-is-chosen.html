WHAT WILL BE GENERATED
A DOI, on publish
A concept DOI for the dataset and a version DOI for v1.0. Minted only when visibility becomes Public — not at upload.
A SHA-256 checksum per file
Computed in your browser during upload and re-verified server-side. Re-checked quarterly for the life of the dataset.
A tabular profile and a codebook stub
Column types, ranges and missingness, so reviewers can read the shape without downloading 2 GB.
A personal-data scan
Runs at step 5, before anything can be made Public. Flags direct identifiers and quasi-identifiers for you to confirm.
