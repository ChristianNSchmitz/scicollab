TIER 3 · PRIVATE · ELIGIBILITY-GATED

Early-career researchers

PhD students, postdocs and research staff within eight years of their first degree. Group leaders and anyone with hiring authority cannot join, cannot read it, and cannot see who is in it.
WHY THE EXCLUSION IS ABSOLUTE
A junior researcher asking "is my supervisor's behaviour normal?" or "should I leave this lab?" cannot ask it in a room their PI might be reading. A space that is mostly juniors is not a safe space; it is a space where you check the member list before you type. Eligibility is verified against the institutional record, and the room is invisible to anyone ineligible.
