WHAT A REQUEST MUST STATE
01The technique, from the controlled vocabulary
02The organism or system
03A real date, or "no fixed date" — not "urgent"

FIELD 04 IS THE WHOLE DESIGN
Collaboration boards fail when they fill with people asking for unpaid work. Requiring the offer up front turns a request into a proposal, and lets the reader decide in ten seconds instead of a three-email negotiation.
EXPIRY IS AUTOMATIC
A request disappears from the board at its deadline whether or not it was matched. Nothing here rots — a board of stale asks is worse than an empty one.
