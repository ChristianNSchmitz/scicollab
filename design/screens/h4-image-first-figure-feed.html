THE TWO RULES THIS FEED ENFORCES
01Underlying data must be linked. A figure with no attached data cannot be posted here at all. The mitigation for aesthetic selection bias is to make the beautiful image inseparable from the numbers behind it.
02Integrity screening runs on upload. Duplicate-region and splice detection, the same checks as the manuscript integrity screen. Findings are advisory and go to the poster first, never public.
AND WHAT IS ABSENT
No filters. No colour presets. No "enhance". The upload path offers exactly one adjustment — a linear brightness match — and records it in the metadata where a reader can see it.
An image tool that makes a gel look better is an image tool for committing misconduct. There is no such control anywhere in this product.
