THE HONEST NOTE · 2 UNSOLVED IN PRINCIPLE
The molecular and genomic viewers are interactive 3D and dense sequence surfaces. We provide a full data table and a text summary for both, but we do not claim the interactive view itself is equivalent for a non-sighted user. We are not aware of a solved approach in any comparable product, and we would rather say so than tick a box.
