WHY A LADDER AT ALL
A brand-new account that can downvote and edit is a moderation problem. Graduated privileges are the cheapest defence, and the thresholds are published so nobody has to guess.
WHAT A LADDER MUST NOT BECOME
No privilege here changes what other people see of your science. Points buy you responsibility inside the platform, never authority over someone else's result.
