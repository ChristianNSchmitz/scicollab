Access, cost and getting out

Screens 11–14. Controlled access from both sides, storage cost stated in euros rather than gigabytes, one-click deposit to the repositories a funder already recognises, and the archival export that makes leaving possible. The last one is the most important thing on this board: an institution will not put its lab knowledge somewhere it cannot get it out of.
