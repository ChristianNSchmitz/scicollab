The new flow does not ask for fewer fields. It asks for three instead of six, because three were readable from the files — and it never makes anyone guess what is coming.
THE ONE RULE
Nothing is required at step n that could have been stated at step 1. Every requirement in this flow appears twice: once as a promise on the contract page, and once as the field itself. When those two disagree, the contract page is the bug.
THE ONE HONEST EXCEPTION
Step 5 cannot be answered before the files exist — a scanner needs something to scan. So step 1 states that the scan will run, what it looks for, and that it can block a change to Public. The surprise is removed even though the step cannot move.
