THE THREE ROUTES, AND WHY ALL THREE EXIST
Warm introduction — highest response rate, needs a shared connection. Available to Alex here because she follows R. Nakamura.
Collaboration request — no relationship needed, states the offer up front, and works at project scale. The route she took.
Office hours — lowest friction of all, but only some people offer them.
A cold direct message is deliberately the least prominent of the four options on screen 6. It is the one that works worst and annoys most.

WHAT THIS FLOW REFUSES TO DO
