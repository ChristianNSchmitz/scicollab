THE THREE THINGS SHE ASKED FOR, ANSWERED
Governance model → the policy defaults table with its override column. Four of six locked by the institution, and a researcher cannot loosen them.
Audit logs → hash-chained, streaming, DENIED retained, with an auditor's screen built around saved questions rather than a search box.
SSO readiness → SAML, eduGAIN, test connection, SCIM with a dry run, and the leaver case decided by policy in advance.
WHAT SELLS IT IS NOT ON THE LIST
Her stated pain is "tacit knowledge leaves when researchers leave". The screen that closes the deal is the handover pack — 94% compiled automatically, six things only the leaver knows, six generated interview questions. Nobody puts that on an RFP because nobody believes it can be bought.
THE DEMO RULE
Every screen in this ribbon shows something unflattering: the weak lab, the failed criterion, the DENIED rows, the 34% appeal overturn rate. That is deliberate. A governance buyer who catches you hiding one number stops believing the other twelve.
