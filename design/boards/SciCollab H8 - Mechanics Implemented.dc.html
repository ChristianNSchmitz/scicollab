WHY THIS IS THE STRONGEST ONE
Replication is chronically under-incentivised everywhere in science — it is unglamorous, unfunded and unpublishable. A mechanic that makes six labs commit to it, and credits the failure identically to the success, is doing something no journal currently does.
MITIGATIONS BUILT IN

A negative outcome scores identically to a positive one
Committing costs nothing and withdrawing is silent
Results attach to the card as failure modes, not as a scoreboard

READ ROW 7 — THAT IS THE WHOLE DESIGN
The most accurate submission sits seventh. It wins on the metric and loses on everything that makes a result usable: nobody has reproduced it, there is no protocol, and it was only ever tested on one line. Under a Kaggle-style board it would be first, and the field would learn nothing from it.
FOUR AXES, EQUALLY WEIGHTED
Accuracy — against a held-out set, as usual
Reproduced — how many independent labs ran your card and got your number
Documented — conditions and failure range stated
Generalises — how many systems it was tested on
Three of the four cannot be optimised alone — they require other people to act, which is what makes them expensive to game.
WHAT THE FIELD GAINS
CASP-style challenges genuinely moved structure prediction. The failure mode was always that the winning method was a black box nobody could run. Requiring the submission to be a method card fixes exactly that, and leaves 31 runnable protocols behind when the challenge closes.
PRIZE
Credit and a citable challenge record — no reputation transfer, no cash. The workbook's caution about wealthy labs buying attention applies to bounties; here there is nothing to buy.

WEIGHTED BY THE ENDORSER
An endorsement from someone with accepted answers in that technique carries several times the weight of one from someone with none. Endorsing outside your own evidenced areas contributes almost nothing.
CAPPED AND DECAYING
Five endorsements given per month, maximum. Unused ones do not bank. An endorsement decays if the person stops doing the thing — so the number tracks current practice, not a 2019 job title.
THE ANTI-RECIPROCITY RULE
If A endorses B and B endorses A within 30 days, both are weighted at zero and both are told why. Reciprocal inflation is the failure mode that made LinkedIn endorsements meaningless; it is cheap to detect and worth detecting.
The count is never shown as a bare number. It is always weight plus who, because "41 endorsements" is a popularity figure and "4 endorsers, 3 with accepted answers in this technique" is evidence.

WHAT YOU MAY COMMIT TO
Publishing a result whatever it shows. Running a replication and recording either outcome. Depositing data on publication. All three are commitments against your own future bias — the kind of promise that actually improves the record.
What you may not commit to: a submission date, a paper count, or anything with a countdown. Those punish the experiment that took longer, which is most of them, and they were the reason to refuse this mechanic in the first place.

K12 SMART BATCHING — THE USER OWNS THE TIMING 700 × 520 · host: G4 notifications
DELIVERY TIMING you set it · we do not learn it
WHEN SHOULD A BATCH ARRIVE?
Start of my day · 08:30 Twice · 08:30 and 16:00 Hourly
Batching is real and it is the good half of "smart": four notifications become one, at a time you nominated. The batch never arrives outside quiet hours and never exceeds three a day.
WHAT WE STILL WILL NOT DO
Learn your open-rate curve and fire at the moment you are most likely to look. That is optimisation for engagement wearing the word "smart", and it converts a tool into a slot machine. The setting above is a preference, not a model.
One exception, and it is stated: a retraction affecting your cited work bypasses batching and quiet hours. It is the only interrupt in the product, and it exists because being wrong overnight is worse than being woken.
