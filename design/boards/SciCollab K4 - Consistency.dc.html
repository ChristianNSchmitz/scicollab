CHECK 5 · FAIL DOES EVERY INTERACTIVE ELEMENT HAVE A VISIBLE FOCUS STATE? measured: style-focus and outline:2px solid occurrences per board
Focus states are specified in the component library and carried into none of the 162 screens.
The atoms board defines focus-visible correctly: 2px --signal at 2px offset, flipping to --ink on the orange primary button, and it is shown for every atom. Every one of the 53 artboards then renders its buttons, inputs, chips and links with hover states only. A grep for style-focus across all screen boards returns zero.
WHY THIS IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS
S01 is a legal requirement under the European Accessibility Act, harmonised standard EN 301 549, applicable since 28 June 2025. J3 audits three screens against WCAG 2.1 AA and traces their keyboard paths properly — but three screens is not the product. Anyone implementing from these boards will build 162 screens with no focus ring, because that is what the boards show.
THE HONEST MITIGATION
These are static artboards, not a running application — a mock has no focus to render. The correct fix is not to add rings to 162 pictures; it is to make the rule impossible to omit in implementation: a single global :focus-visible rule in the base stylesheet, plus a line in the handoff stating that no component may override it. That is one line of CSS and one line of policy, and it is worth more than a thousand annotated mocks.

WHAT THE AUDIT SAYS ABOUT THE SYSTEM
The rules that were semantic — the visibility chip, the status palette, sources on AI — held across 53 boards with almost no drift. The rules that were quantitative or invisible — a budget of three, a focus ring — did not. A designer can see a missing chip; nobody can see a missing focus state or count to three across an artboard.
THE THREE FIXES
1. One global :focus-visible rule in the base stylesheet, unoverridable by any component.
2. E3 viewers: data traces move off --signal onto a neutral ramp.
3. I2 dashboards: visibility chip on the department and integrity tables.
AND ONE RULE TO REWRITE
"Max three orange elements" is unenforceable as written — three per what? Rewrite as "at most one orange primary action and one orange status indicator per viewport", which is checkable by looking at a screen.
