THE THREE VERSIONS
Submitted — your manuscript before review
Accepted — after peer review, before typesetting
Published — the publisher's typeset PDF
Publishers grant different rights to each. Most disputes are about people sharing the third when they may only share the second.

This publisher permits private sharing between named individuals but prohibits public posting of any version. Request-a-copy is the lawful path: a person asks, you decide, the file goes to that person.
WHAT A REQUESTER SEES
Title, authors, abstract and DOI — always public. Then a Request a copy button that e-mails you. No automated fulfilment, because the permission is yours to give.

The file is uploaded now and flips to public automatically on the date. Nobody has to diarise it, and nobody has to remember in eleven months' time.
YOUR FUNDER DISAGREES WITH THIS EMBARGO
FWO requires open access without embargo. Your institution's rights retention policy may override the publisher's 12 months — check with the research office before accepting the delay.
WHY THIS SCREEN EXISTS · AND WHY IT IS NOT OPTIONAL
A platform that lets researchers upload publisher PDFs without checking rights is not a neutral host — it is a defendant. In 2023 ResearchGate settled with the American Chemical Society and Elsevier and agreed to implement exactly this: automated checks against publisher policy, with sharing limited to the version and audience the policy actually permits. We are designing that mechanism in from the first release, because retrofitting it means asking hundreds of thousands of users to accept a capability being removed.
THE DESIGN RULES THAT FOLLOW
The check runs on upload, not on publish — you never hold an unlawful file.
The outcome is a permanent visible state on the paper, not a one-time dialogue.
When the policy is ambiguous, the answer is the more restrictive of the two readings, with the ambiguity named.
The policy source and retrieval date are always shown, because policies change.

WHAT THE LINKAGE FIXES
Without it, the preprint accumulates citations as a separate work and readers land on a version 18 months out of date. With it, every manifestation carries a banner pointing at the version of record, and the metrics are reported together and separately.
