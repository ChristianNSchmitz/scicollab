HOW AN ISSUE CLOSES
Four outcomes only: fixed (a method-card version), explained (an accepted answer), published as a null result, or abandoned with a stated reason. There is no "won't fix" and no silent close.
WHY NOT JUST TASKS
A task is something a person owes. An issue is something the lab does not yet understand. Conflating them gives you a to-do list and no record of what was learned.
