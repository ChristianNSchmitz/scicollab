PREPRINT · bioRxiv · 2026-07-02
Receptor abundance predicts lipid-mediated transfection efficiency across passage
Nakamura R, Devries H, Marchetti L · 10.1101/2026.07.02.601188
RESULTS · PARAGRAPH 3
Transfection efficiency declined monotonically with passage number in all three lines tested. The decline was steepest between passage 16 and passage 20, where mean efficiency fell from 38.4% to 6.1%, consistent with a threshold effect rather than gradual attrition. Surface receptor abundance measured by flow cytometry fell in parallel (r = 0.91, p < 0.001, n = 27), and receptor blockade in low-passage cells reproduced the high-passage phenotype. Restoring receptor expression by transient overexpression recovered efficiency to 31.2%, though not to baseline. We did not test whether the effect generalises to non-lipid delivery.

THE RULES OF THE QUEUE
Three approvals to apply an edit. Every decision is signed. Rejections require a reason that the proposer reads. Nobody reviews their own artifact, and safety flags leave the community queue entirely.
WHAT WE ARE WATCHING FOR
A review queue is where a community turns hostile. Rejections carry a reason, closures name their voters, and reviewer decisions are visible on the reviewer's own record — accountability runs both ways.
