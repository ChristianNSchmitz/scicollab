THE TWO CATASTROPHIC FINDINGS both are error-prevention failures, both are one-way doors
H5 · ERROR PREVENTIONE1 deposit · step 4 licence picker
A licence choice is permanent and the screen does not say so
Once a dataset is public under CC-BY, that grant cannot be withdrawn from anyone who already has the data. The licence select on deposit step 4 looks and behaves exactly like the format select two fields above it — same control, same weight, no confirmation, no statement of irreversibility. The patent gate gets a full modal with type-to-confirm; a licence grant is the same class of one-way door and gets a dropdown.
Fix: a one-line irreversibility statement under the control, and a confirmation step when the choice is more permissive than the project default. Not a modal — the patent gate earns one because it is once per project; this fires on every deposit.
H1 · VISIBILITY OF SYSTEM STATUSD1 ELN · entry view
Append-only is stated in words but never shown while typing
The notebook entry explains the append-only constraint in a panel, but the composing area gives no persistent signal that what is typed becomes immutable on save. A researcher who assumes normal draft-and-edit behaviour will commit a half-finished entry, and in a countersigned regulated notebook that entry cannot be removed — only superseded, visibly, forever.
Fix: a permanent chrome state on the editor itself — a left rule and a label reading APPEND-ONLY · SAVE IS FINAL — plus an explicit draft mode that is visibly not the notebook until committed.
