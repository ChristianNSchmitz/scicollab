MUTEsilent, reversible, one-sided
You stop seeing them. They can still see and answer your public work. Nothing is announced. For someone whose volume is tiring, not someone who is a problem.
BLOCKmutual, immediate, silent to them
No messages, no comments on your artifacts, no connection requests, no visibility of your non-public work. They are not told. Existing co-authorship and citations are untouched — blocking is a communication boundary, never a rewriting of the record.
REPORTgoes to humans, with a stated route
Six categories. Spam and harassment go to platform trust and safety. Research misconduct goes to the institution's named integrity office, not to community moderators — that is a formal process with legal weight and it does not belong in a volunteer queue.
TWO CLICKS, EVERY SURFACE
Person card, message header, comment, answer, community post, event thread. Same menu, same position. A safety control that has to be hunted for does not exist.
RATE LIMITS
Unsolicited messages: 5/day for a new account, 20/day established. Connection requests: 10/day. Applied silently — a spammer should not learn the threshold.
AFTER YOU REPORT
You are told what happened and when. A report that vanishes into silence teaches people not to report.
