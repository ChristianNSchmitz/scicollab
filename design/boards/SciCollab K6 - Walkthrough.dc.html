DIFFERENTIATOR 4 OF 5

The patentability gate

BEFORE YOU MAKE THIS PUBLIC first public posting from this project
Publishing this destroys your ability to patent it in Europe.
Under the European Patent Convention, any public disclosure before filing becomes prior art against your own invention. There is no general grace period in Europe — unlike the United States, the loss is immediate and cannot be undone by filing later.
Keep private · talk to the TTO first
type PUBLISH to confirm
Publish anyway
Loss of patent rights through premature disclosure is one of the most expensive mistakes in academic research — and it is always made by someone who did not know the rule.
THE ASYMMETRY IS DELIBERATE
The reversible choice is one click and full width. The irreversible one requires typing a word — not to obstruct, but to turn an automatic click into a decision the person remembers making. Both outcomes are written to the audit log.
Shown once per project, not per file. A gate that appears on every upload is one people learn to dismiss without reading.

RESTRAINT

What this product refuses to do

each one is a positioning decision, not an omission
NO SINGLE SCORE
Six reputation axes that cannot be summed. We refused B20's field-normalised percentile outright — a percentile is a single score in disguise.
NO INDIVIDUAL METRICS
Per-person data is not computed, not permission-gated. A researcher who thinks this feeds their appraisal stops recording honest null results within a month.
NO AI VERDICTS
Every AI output carries clickable sources, states what it did not find, and has no accept button. An AI cannot resolve a thread.
NO ENGAGEMENT TRICKS
No infinite scroll, no like counts, no re-engagement email. Streaks and leaderboards ship off, carrying the argument against themselves on screen.
NO TRAINING ON PRIVATE WORK
Not a toggle — there is no circumstance in which we do it. Public artifacts are opt-in and default off, which costs us a worse index at launch.
AND THE ONE WE REVERSED
Eight engagement mechanics were first refused, then built — on the argument that engagement aimed at replication and reuse is a field-level instrument. Each now points at a verifiable act.
WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

Four decisions block the build

twelve open questions in total · K5
Q2
CC0 or CC BY for external contributions?
Every contribution made before this is settled inherits the wrong default and cannot be relicensed without contacting each contributor. My view: CC BY.
Q4
Are ephemeral lab stories compatible with a permanent record?
The one feature that contradicts our own thesis. My view: keep the low-friction capture, drop the 24-hour expiry. Same posting cost, no contradiction.
Q6
Pseudonymous mode for sensitive questions?
The trust model rests on verified identity; the most valuable questions cannot be asked under a real name. My view: platform knows, community does not.
Q9
Can an institution ever obtain per-researcher metrics?
Not a design question. If one institution gets per-person data and researchers learn of it, the honesty of the corpus is gone and cannot be rebuilt. My view: write it into the terms as a binding commitment, not a setting.
