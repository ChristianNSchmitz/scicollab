WHY THERE ARE NO STREAK REWARDS
A daily-streak reward in a research context pays people for logging in, not for thinking. The map shows a streak because it is a fact about the data; nothing is awarded for it, and breaking one costs nothing.
OPT-OUT · OFF STATE
Show this on my profile
Hidden from your profile and from institutional dashboards. You keep the map privately. Nobody is told it was hidden.
WHAT MANAGERS CANNOT DO
Institutional dashboards can show aggregate reuse for a lab. They cannot show a per-person contribution map, whatever the plan. This is a product rule, not a setting.
