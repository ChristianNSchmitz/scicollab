The band is deliberate. Below GitHub the product looks like a toy to a procurement office; above protocols.io it needs to displace an incumbent rather than sit beside one. Publishing the comparison is itself a positioning decision — a research tool that hides its price signals that the price depends on how much it thinks you can pay.
THE LINE WE DO NOT CROSS
Reading is never paid. Publishing a null result is never paid. Citing, forking and reproducing are never paid. We charge for private storage, lab operations and institutional governance — never for participation in the scientific record.
NO PER-DOI, NO APC
Charging per DOI would make researchers ration what they publish, and the whole product depends on them not doing that.
SEATS, NOT USAGE
Usage-based pricing on a research platform means a PI discouraging students from uploading data. A predictable per-seat line is also the only thing a grant budget can absorb.
