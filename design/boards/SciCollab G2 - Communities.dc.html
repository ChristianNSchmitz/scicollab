WHY THE TIERS LOOK DIFFERENT AND NOT JUST LABELLED DIFFERENTLY
The failure this prevents is a researcher pasting an unpublished result into a tier-1 forum believing it was tier 3. A text label is read once and then invisible. Border weight, ground colour and an icon are visible in peripheral vision, survive greyscale, and are present in every card, every thread header and the composer itself.

11 EARLY-CAREER RESEARCHER SPACE (C21) 620 × 680 · no PIs, and it says so
TIER 3 · PRIVATE · ELIGIBILITY-GATED

Early-career researchers

PhD students, postdocs and research staff within eight years of their first degree. Group leaders and anyone with hiring authority cannot join, cannot read it, and cannot see who is in it.
WHY THE EXCLUSION IS ABSOLUTE
A junior researcher asking "is my supervisor's behaviour normal?" or "should I leave this lab?" cannot ask it in a room their PI might be reading. A space that is mostly juniors is not a safe space; it is a space where you check the member list before you type. Eligibility is verified against the institutional record, and the room is invisible to anyone ineligible.

PEER SUPPORT WITH ESCALATION · C26
Threads touching harassment, safety or serious distress carry a permanent banner: this room is peers, not professionals, with the institution's confidential channels and a national helpline listed. Moderators can escalate a thread to that route; they cannot counsel.
