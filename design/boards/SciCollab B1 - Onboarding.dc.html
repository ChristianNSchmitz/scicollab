FOUR THINGS, ORGANISED
01
Artifact-rich questions

A question is never plain text. It carries the data, the code, the images and the context that produced it.

02
Structured metadata

Method, conditions, outcomes — captured as a first-class object, not a paragraph in a PDF.

03
Provenance

Versioning and timestamps on every artifact. Who changed what, when, and what it looked like before.

04
Retrieval

Find the relevant prior case — and the person behind it. AI surfaces and structures; the community validates.

FIRST-CLASS OBJECT
Negative and null results

Not a footnote. Not a graveyard section. A citable, reusable object with the same weight as a positive result — and its own scored axis on your profile. Orange, deliberately not red: a failure that reproduces is a contribution.

FIRST-CLASS OBJECT
Method Cards

Structured, versioned, forkable records of how something was actually done — including the conditions under which it failed. Fork one, run it, and your result attaches to the card whether it worked or not.
