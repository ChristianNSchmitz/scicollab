14 INTEGRITY CHECKS (J17, J18, J19) — ADVISORY, NEVER ACCUSATORY 1440 × 660 · canvas region
LAB visible to the authors only · not sent to anyone, not attached to the submission, not stored as a score MS-0041/Checks
These are things worth a second look, not findings of misconduct.
Every automated integrity tool produces false positives at a rate that makes an unreviewed verdict dangerous to a career. So this screen states what was detected, why it might be innocent, and what a human should check — and the result is never sent anywhere without an author's action.

SIMILARITY · J171 PASSAGE
"Cells were maintained at 37 °C in 5% CO₂ and passaged at 80% confluence using 0.25% trypsin-EDTA."
28 words · matches your own MC-0288 v6 and 214 other papers
Why this is probably fine: standard methods language has a limited number of correct phrasings, and this passage matches your own method card. Copying yourself is not plagiarism.
What to check: that the method card is cited, so a reader knows where the wording comes from.
IMAGE INTEGRITY · J18LOOK AT THIS
Fig 3a · panel 2
Fig 3c · panel 1
What was detected: a 34 × 30 px region appears in both panels with a 0.94 structural similarity.
Why this may be innocent: both panels are crops from the same acquisition field, which is normal and not a duplication — but it must be stated in the legend.
What to check: whether the legend says both crops come from one field. If they came from different experiments, that is a real problem to resolve now.
STATISTICAL SANITY · J191 NOTE
reported · t(28) = 2.31, p = 0.014
recomputed · t(28) = 2.31, p = 0.028
two-tailed vs one-tailed
What was detected: the reported p-value corresponds to a one-tailed test; the methods section says two-tailed.
Why this may be innocent: a directional hypothesis was pre-registered, which justifies one-tailed — the methods text simply has not been updated.
What to check: PREREG-0044 states a directional hypothesis. Either fix the methods sentence or the p-value — the two must agree.
WHAT THIS SCREEN WILL NEVER DO
Produce a percentage. Rank you against other authors. Notify your PI, your institution or a journal. Store a permanent "integrity score" on your profile. Every one of those turns a noisy signal into a reputational weapon, and the false-positive rate does not support it.
WHO SEES IT AND WHEN
Only the authors, while the manuscript is a draft. If you choose to, the run can be attached to a submission as evidence that you checked — a decision you make, not a disclosure the platform makes for you.
