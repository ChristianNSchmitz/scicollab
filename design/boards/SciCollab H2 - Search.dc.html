WHY FOUR ITEMS AND NOT TWENTY
A digest that lists everything is a digest nobody opens twice. Each of the four earns its slot against a different criterion: changes your plan, changes something you depend on, tells you your work was used, something only you can do.
THE WORKBOOK'S WARNING, APPLIED
Notifications are named as "the primary lever of compulsive use", with hard caps, batching and no artificial urgency as mitigations. One weekly email, no urgency language, no unread counters, and three ways to turn it down in the footer rather than buried in settings.
RENDERS AT 600px
Single column, no background images, borders rather than shadows, and legible as plain text if images are blocked — which in an institutional mail client they usually are.
