THE GAP LIST · 13 FEATURES NOT DESIGNED not padded · each reason is one we would defend out loud
ID
FEATURE
WHY NOT
J30
Registered report workflow
Genuine omission, and the one that should embarrass us. Registered reports are the strongest available answer to publication bias, and this product's whole thesis is negative results. It belonged in batch F.
R10
Help centre and support channel
Genuine omission. No defence — never scheduled into a batch. Also the cheapest screen on this list to build.
A20
Pseudonymous mode
Blocked on a policy decision. The trust model rests on ORCID-verified identity; pseudonymity is genuinely valuable for whistle-blowing and sensitive questions. Both cannot be true by default.
F25
Post in your own language, translated
Blocked on safety. Machine-translating a protocol can alter a concentration or a timing. Needs a human-in-the-loop design and belongs with the S-domain work.
D06
Video and voice calls
Declined. Commodity infrastructure every lab already has. A second-rate Zoom inside a research tool spends roadmap on the one thing nobody asked for.
D07
Voice notes with transcription
Declined, same reason — plus an unsearchable audio blob in a record-keeping product is an anti-feature.
G28
Effort and time tracking per work package
Declined by design. Per-person time tracking contradicts the no-individual-metrics rule every dashboard enforces. Grant reporting needs it — we would build it at project level only.
B08
Written recommendations
Declined. LinkedIn-style testimonials are reciprocity theatre. The weighted endorsement (K11) and the reputation axes carry the same signal with evidence behind them.
A15
Vanity URL and public handle
Deferred. ORCID already is the persistent identifier; a second namespace adds squatting and impersonation surface for a cosmetic gain.
A16
Conference card / QR code
Deferred. Small and pleasant; lower value than everything that beat it for space in J1.
A24
Per-user inbound e-mail alias
Deferred. Bypasses the reputation gate and the spam quarantine, both load-bearing. Needs its own abuse design first.
I16
Internal package registry
Deferred. Institutions with this need already run Artifactory or similar; integrating beats rebuilding.
I19
Snippet library
Deferred. Substantially overlaps the method card at code granularity; needs a boundary rule before it earns a screen.

WHAT A 100% BOARD WOULD HAVE COST
Thirteen more screens, four of which we would have had to argue against ourselves the moment anyone asked why they existed. A coverage index whose only purpose is to be green is one nobody checks twice.
RECOMMENDED NEXT BATCH
Not the biggest gap — the most consequential. J30 registered reports, R10 help centre, and the D-domain messaging partials. Together roughly nine screens, and they close the only two omissions on this board we cannot justify.
