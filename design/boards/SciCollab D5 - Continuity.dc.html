WHAT TUNDE KEEPS
His authorship, his CRediT roles, his reputation axes and his profile — all of it travels to Karolinska. A handover transfers custody, never credit. Making departure feel like confiscation is how a lab loses the next person's cooperation too.

THE REAL FAILURE MODE
Field data is lost not because sync fails, but because someone did not realise they were offline and assumed it had uploaded. Every decision on this screen is aimed at that, not at the network.
WHY CAPTURE IS A TAB
The phone exists to get an observation, a photo or a scanned sample into a project in under 30 seconds. Authoring — method cards, analysis, publication — is desktop work, and the mobile shell does not pretend otherwise.
