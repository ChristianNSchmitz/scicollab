RESTRICT, DO NOT DELETE
A contested artifact is set to Private, never destroyed, until the process finishes. Deletion during a dispute makes the appeal meaningless and is irreversible if the complaint turns out to be wrong.
COUNTER-NOTICE · N06
A takedown notice must identify the work, the rightsholder and a legal basis, and carries a statement of truth. Abusive or repeated bad-faith notices cost the sender their ability to file — the mechanism cuts both ways, and it says so on the submission form.

NEVER IN THIS TIER
Harassment, safety, personal data, research misconduct, legal notices. Asking a volunteer postdoc to adjudicate a harassment report is unfair to them and to the reporter.

THE WORK SURVIVES THE ACCOUNT
Termination ends participation. It does not retract published datasets or remove authorship — a scientific record altered as a disciplinary act is a falsified record.
ESCALATION IS NOT AUTOMATIC
Three warnings do not become a ban by arithmetic. Each rung requires a fresh decision with reasons, and rungs 4 and 5 are appealable to an external body.
Published in advance, in full. A code of conduct written after an incident is a justification; written before one it is a contract. The ladder, the categories and the appeal route are all on the public page.
