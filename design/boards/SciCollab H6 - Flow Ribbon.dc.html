THE ORDER IS THE ARGUMENT
Every consumer product would put the AI box first — it demos beautifully and it is what users ask for. Putting it fourth is the whole thesis: AI is good at summarising a corpus you have already judged, and bad at judging one for you. Steps 6 and 7 are where the judgement happens, and they are human, fast and explicit.
